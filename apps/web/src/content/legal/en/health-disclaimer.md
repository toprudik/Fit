## 1. DaFit is not medical advice

DaFit is software for trainers and their clients, run by [OPERATOR FULL NAME], an individual, at [ADDRESS] ("DaFit", "we", "us"). Words such as trainer, client, questionnaire and showcase have the meaning given in the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md), section 2. Nothing in DaFit is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and DaFit is not a medical device. Do not use DaFit instead of a doctor, dietitian or other health professional.

## 2. Numbers in DaFit are estimates

2.1. Calorie and macronutrient targets are calculated with the Mifflin-St Jeor formula from your sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal. They are a starting point for an average healthy adult, not a prescription. They do not take into account medical conditions, medicines, pregnancy or breastfeeding.

2.2. Food values come from the open Open Food Facts database and from what users enter. They can be wrong or incomplete. Steps, sleep and other figures are only as accurate as the device or the person that recorded them.

2.3. Targets are not calculated for people under 18, and the Service is for adults only.

## 3. Your trainer is not your doctor

3.1. Your trainer provides their own services and is responsible for the programmes, workouts, homework and advice they give you. DaFit does not write, check or approve them.

3.2. A trainer is not a doctor, physiotherapist or dietitian unless they say so on their showcase and are qualified and allowed to practise as one where they work. A verified badge means only that DaFit looked at the documents the trainer sent.

3.3. The questionnaire (intake form) and its PAR-Q questions help your trainer understand your health. A flag on your answers is a prompt to talk to a doctor, not a diagnosis. Your trainer sees your questionnaire only if you choose to show it.

## 4. See a doctor first if

- you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, asthma, a joint or back problem, or any other long-term condition;
- you have had chest pain, fainting or dizziness;
- you are pregnant, have recently given birth, or are breastfeeding;
- you take medicines that affect your heart rate, blood pressure or blood sugar;
- you have or have had an eating disorder;
- you answered "yes" to any PAR-Q question;
- you are not sure whether exercise or a change in diet is safe for you.

## 5. Stop if something feels wrong

Stop exercising straight away and get help if you feel pain, especially in your chest, arm, neck or jaw, or if you feel faint, dizzy, very short of breath, or sick. Tell your trainer. Do not continue a programme that hurts you because an app or a plan says so.

## 6. Emergencies

DaFit is not an emergency service and nobody watches the app in real time. In an emergency, call your local emergency number: **112** in the EU and many other countries, **911** in the US and Canada, **999** or **112** in the UK, **103** or **112** in Russia.

## 7. Your choices

You decide whether to follow a programme or a target. Go at a pace that is safe for you.
