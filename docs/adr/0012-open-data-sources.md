# 12. Open data for foods and exercises

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-13

No free, good Russian food database exists, and licensed ones cost money. Foods come from **Open Food Facts** (open database, barcodes, decent Russian coverage) through a server-side search proxy with a cache, plus users' own foods and quick kcal entries; our private foods are never pushed back to OFF. Exercises are seeded from **wger** (CC-BY-SA) with RU/EN names; attribution is shown in the app and the derived exercise library stays under a compatible license. Paid alternatives (licensed food DBs, AI photo recognition) are post-beta.
